Common questions

How do the ranks work?

There are seven, from Beginner to Sensei. Beginner gives you a 500 millisecond window around the target; Sensei gives you 50. Land ten clean rounds in a row and you move up a rank. Miss three in a row and you drop back one.

Why can't I play Blind, Ghost, or Survival yet?

They unlock in order. One round of Classic unlocks Blind, one round of Blind unlocks Ghost, and one round of Ghost unlocks Survival. The app tells you the moment each one opens.

Why did Survival stop letting me play?

Survival is limited to three runs a day. The count resets at midnight, your local time. Every other mode is unlimited.

How does the daily streak work?

Complete five rounds before the day rolls over and the day counts. Miss a whole day and the streak resets — unless you have a Streak Freeze banked, which covers exactly one missed day. You earn a freeze by clearing all three daily quests in a day.

What is Time IQ?

A 20 question diagnostic mixing all three timing formats under one fixed tolerance, so every attempt is comparable to your last. You can retake it as often as you like; only your best score is kept.

Do I need an account?

No. The game works fully without one. Signing in with Apple is optional and exists only so your progress survives changing or resetting your device.

I changed phone and lost my progress

Progress lives on the device unless you signed in with Apple. If you did, sign in on the new device with the same Apple ID and it comes back. If you didn't, unfortunately it can't be recovered — sign in on the new device to protect it from here on.

How do I delete my data?

Email the address above, either from the address associated with your account or including your in-game display name, and your account and its scores will be deleted. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device immediately. Details are in the privacy policy.
